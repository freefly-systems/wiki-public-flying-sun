# Flight Part 3: Landing and Pack up

#### Landing:

1. Dimming the lights before landing is recommended to avoid blinding the pilot.
2. Aircraft should land where it launched.
3. Aircraft should land with no tension in the cable.
   1. As the aircraft descend and fly closer to tether, the tension in cable will be released.

#### Pack Up:

1. After disarming, disable HV from the app or the screen and confirm it’s off.
2. Turn off the SL8, then disconnect the LP12 and the strain relief.
3. Retract the cable: hold Take Up in the app and release it before the plug reaches the ground unit.

{% hint style="info" %}
Take Up only works with HV off.
{% endhint %}

1. Check the cable isn’t kinked or knotted as it retracts.
2. Turn off the backup power switch, then the main power switch.
3. Disconnect AC input from the power source and pack up.
