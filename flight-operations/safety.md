# Safety

## Operational Hazards

### High voltage

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (6).png" alt="" width="188"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Beware of tether cable and Air Unit during operation, do not touch tether cable, tether air unit, or FS500 aircraft when HV is enabled.&#x20;

### Hot Surface&#x20;

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (7).png" alt="" width="188"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Beware of Light panels and Air unit.

### Bright Light&#x20;

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (8).png" alt="" width="188"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Beware of bright light while being under the drone. Sun glasses are included in FS500 case.&#x20;



## Potential In-Flight Disruptions

Pilots should be aware of potential disruptions in-flight, and be prepared to take appropriate actions

| Disruption                                     | Cause                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Resolution                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Tether overcurrent**                         | When tether over currents, it may shut down, and Flying Sun will rely on a single SL8. AMC will announce "battery failsafe", and tether ground unit will alarm.                                                            | In the event of tether overcurrent, aircraft will return to launch after a 5 second hold. During which pilot can switch to position mode and land manually if they choose so.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Tether overextension**                       | The tether cable is 110 m long. The ground unit alarm sounds above 95 m of extension and stops below 95 m.                                                                                                                 | In the event of tether overextension, slowly fly back towards the tether until tether alarm stops.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Tether oscillation**                         | Tether ground unit actively keeps cable tension by regulating the retraction torque.                                                                                                                                       | In the event of tether oscillation, increase aircrafts altitude to create tension in cable. Do not hover still.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Power source shutdown**                      | If the power source of the tether ground unit shuts down, the tether will stop supplying high voltage to the aircraft, aircraft will rely on a single SL8. At the same time tether Ground Unit will sound a beeping alarm. | In the event of power source shutdown, aircraft will return to launch after a 5 second hold. During which pilot can switch to position mode and land manually if they choose so.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Flying Sun App losing connection to tether** | It's important to monitor the tether output power in flying sun app during flight, in some instances the app may lose connection to the tether.                                                                            | If this occurs, point the Pilot Pro towards the tether and wait for reconnection.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Tether entanglement**                        | <p></p><p>While flying a tether drone, always be aware of the tether cable. </p><ul><li>Avoid flying over tall obstacles</li><li>Avoid flying near trees</li><li>Notify other pilots of your tether</li></ul>              | <p></p><p>In the event of tether entanglement:</p><ol><li>Turn off HV via Flying Sun app</li><li>Turn off Ground Unit main breaker and backup power</li><li>Fly FS500 away from the obstacle</li><li>Attempt to land</li><li><p>If landing is impossible, make sure no one is under the drone and press the kill switch on pilot pro</p><ol><li>Contact Freefly and tell us about your experience</li></ol></li></ol> |
| **Precipitation**                              | FS500 is powered by a high voltage tether that should not be used in the rain                                                                                                                                              | In the event of rain, please land and shutdown high voltage immediately.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
