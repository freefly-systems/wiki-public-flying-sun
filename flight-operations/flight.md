# Flight Part 2: Take off and flight

#### Before takeoff

* Make sure all personnel are clear of the aircraft and tether.
* Turn on the ground unit’s main power switch and backup power button.
* Enable HV last, immediately before arming. You can enable it from the Flying Sun app or the ground unit screen.&#x20;

#### Takeoff

* Take off in Position mode or use auto takeoff.
* Ascend directly above take off point, and avoid flying laterally at low altitudes
* The ground unit keeps tension on the tether once the aircraft reaches altitude.
* Keep the tether taut during extended hovers.

#### During flight

* Monitor tether power:
  * Under about 1100W when hovering with the lights off.
  * Under about 2800W when hovering with the lights at 100%.
  * The max limit is 3000W.
* Things that increase power draw: climbing, slowing a descent, raising the lights, high altitude and wind.
* Slightly dimming the lights while climbing gives more power margin.
* Monitor tether extension. The ground unit alarms at 95m.&#x20;
* Avoid hovering at the tether zero point, where the tether can oscillate.
* If BLE drops, the app shows estimated power and length from aircraft telemetry. HV stays in its last state.
* Never leave the controls. Know how to cut the power source and keep a Class D extinguisher on hand.
