# Pro Tips

**App features:**

* The overlay bubble shows tether power, length and an HV toggle on top of AMC, so you don’t need split screen.
* If BLE drops, the app keeps showing estimated power and length, calculated from aircraft telemetry.

**Tether handling:**

* Adjust tension mid-flight: run it lower near the ground for agility and raise it while climbing or in wind at altitude.

{% hint style="info" %}
To adjust tension, swipe down on the ground unit screen to open the Settings menu.
{% endhint %}

* Lay the tether out away from foot and vehicle traffic.
* When manually retracting tether cable during pack up.&#x20;
  * Release Take Up before the plug reaches the ground unit so the cable doesn’t retract too fast.
  * Guide the cable by hand while it retracts. This helps control the retraction speed and lets you inspect the cable for damage.

**Power management:**

* Dim the lights while climbing, then bring them back up at altitude. This avoids hitting the power limit.
* Know your baseline draw (about 1100W with lights off, about 2800W at 100%). A creeping number is an early sign of wind or a problem.
* For long nights on a generator, size the fuel for the full flight plus a buffer.

**Operations:**

* If the tablet has an issue, HV can still be controlled from the ground unit screen.
* Keep sunglasses handy, since the 175,000-lumen panel is blinding at close range.
* Wind at 80m can be much stronger than on the ground, so plan tension and position for that.

**Limitations:**

* Don’t fly in the rain.
* The power limit is 3000W, and the ground unit shuts down above it.
* Tether length is 110m, with the alarm at 95m.
* Estimated values are approximate. Treat them as a backup, not as primary data.
